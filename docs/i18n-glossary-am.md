# Amharic glossary & style guide (draft for approval)

Scope: candidate-facing Mini App only.
Register: **formal-polite, gender-neutral** — second person always plural-polite
(እርስዎ … verbs in plural-polite imperative: ይጀምሩ, ያስገቡ, ይላኩ); third person for
the other participant uses the honourific, gender-neutral **እሳቸው**.
Nothing here is final until approved; every choice lists its alternative.

## A. Brand & core nouns

| # | EN | Proposed AM | Rationale / alternatives |
|---|----|-------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Kidan (wordmark) | **Kidan** (Latin, unchanged) | Brand asset stays Latin in both locales (as Telegram/Facebook do in Amharic UIs); codes stay `KD-…`. Alt: render as ኪዳን in Amharic locale. |
| 2 | introduction (matchmaking) | **ትውውቅ** | The culturally exact word for a mediated introduction toward marriage. |
| 3 | introduction request | **የትውውቅ ጥያቔ** | Compositional and instantly legible. |
| 4 | connection (approved pair) | **ግንኙነት**; tab = **ግንኙነቶች** | Neutral and warm. Alt: ትውውቅ (collides with #2). |
| 5 | private shortlist | **የግል ምርጫ ዝርዝር** (short: **ዝርዝር**) | "Your private list of choices"; never implies the other person was notified. |
| 6 | values-only / values-first | **በእሴት ብቻ** / **እሴት-ትኮር** | እሴት = value; both forms needed (adjective vs principle). |
| 7 | verification photo | **የማረጋግጫ ፎቶ** | ፎቶ is the stable loan. Alt: የማንነት ማረጋግጫ ፎቶ (longer). |
| 8 | review / reviewer / administrator | **ግምገማ / ገማጊ / አስትደደሪ** | "Private review" = **የግል ግምገማ**; "administrator review" = **አስትደደሪ ግምገማ**. |
| 9 | profile (public) / tab | **መገለጫ** | Native term; tab label identical. Alt loan: ፕሮፋዪል. |
| 10 | public code (KD-…) | **የመገለጫ ኮድ** | Ties the anonymous code to the public-profile concept. |
| 11 | bio / about you | **አጭር የግል መገለጫ** | The term itself sets the length expectation. |
| 12 | the other participant | pronoun **እሳቸው**; noun (first mention) **ተዋዋይ** | እሳቸው is honourific and gender-neutral — exactly right pre-reveal. Alt noun አጋርዎ implies couplehood too early. |

## Orthography & typography rules

- Digits: Western (1, 2, 3…) everywhere, ages and quotas included — current Ethiopian digital convention. Dates stay Gregorian; Ethiopian-calendar display is a later, explicit feature.
- Punctuation: Amharic full stop **።**; question mark **?** (modern convention over **፧**); standardise on the Latin comma **,**.
- Loans only where no stable native term exists: ፎቶ፣ ፒሎት፣ ኮድ። Telegram = **ቴሌግራም** in prose, "Telegram" in brand contexts.
- Font stack gains Ethiopic faces: `"Noto Sans Ethiopic", "Abyssinica SIL", "Kefa", "Nyala"` before generic fallbacks; line-height +2% for Ge'ez ascenders; **no letter-spacing** on Amharic text (it breaks conjunct reading).
- No machine-translation leftovers: every string is transcreated in context; each key records its screen and purpose for the reviewer.
