Help

Frequently asked questions

The things we hear most often. Didn't find your answer? Reach the operator from the bot or through Report a concern.

Is Kidan free?
Yes — the current pilot is completely free. If that ever changes, you'll be told in advance, in the app.
What happens to my verification photo?
It is private from the moment you upload it: encrypted, and seen only by the human operator reviewing your profile. It is deleted after review — or, where a small thumbnail is kept to recognise the reviewed account, it is removed within 14 days of approval. It is never shown to other members. Ever.
Will my photo or name ever appear to other members?
No. Discovery shows only an anonymous values-shot profile plus a private reference code (like KD-48213X). Identities are revealed to each other only after both of you confirm an introduction — that's the design, not a setting.
Why does a human review my profile?
Because the trust between members is the whole product. A quick human check keeps out dishonest, copied, or harmful profiles before anyone else ever sees them. Rejected profiles come back with the reason, so you can fix and resubmit.
What is a journey?
A guided, step-by-step way of getting to know someone whose profile interested you. It's a timed sequence of questions that you each answer separately, revealing compatible values before any personal detail appears. If a journey ends with a mutual yes from both of you, the connection is confirmed — and only then is identity exchanged.
Can I see or delete my data?
Both, any time, from the app's Settings (Data section) — you can export a full copy of what's stored about you, or delete your account and data entirely. Our privacy notice explains the details.
Something on the platform concerns me. What do I do?
Use Report a concern on this site — or the Support button in the bot if you have an account. Reports go to a human operator; you don't need to confront anyone yourself, and your privacy is protected while it's handled.
Where does Kidan work? Which languages?
Kidan works anywhere Telegram works. The bot, app, and this hub are currently in English; deeper multilingual support is on the roadmap.
