The rules

Kidan works because everyone in it plays by the same small set of rules. They exist to keep the space honest, respectful, and safe — for you and for everyone else whose story is here.

The short version

  • Be honest and respectful — with the operator and with other members.
  • One person, one profile. No second accounts, no browsing under a borrowed identity.
  • Profiles are reviewed by a human before they appear in discovery.
  • No sharing contact details (phone numbers, Telegram handles, links) before an introduction is mutually confirmed.
  • We remove profiles that break these rules.

In detail

  1. Honesty. Everything you put in your profile must be true — your age, your circumstances, your intentions. Discovering inaccurate information later is grounds for removal.
  2. Respect. Rude, humiliating, or discriminatory language in your profile, in journey answers, or toward the operator is not accepted.
  3. One profile per person. You represent yourself. Creating multiple profiles or applying on someone else's behalf is not allowed.
  4. Review before visibility. Your profile is not visible to anyone until a human operator approves it. A rejected profile comes back with the reason so you can correct it; keep it honest and it will pass quickly.
  5. No contact details early. Phone numbers, Telegram usernames, links, or social handles must not be placed in profile text or journey answers. Identity and contact exchange happen only when both of you have confirmed through the introduction step.
  6. Safety signals are taken seriously. If something about another member concerns you — pressure, dishonesty, anything that feels off — use Report a concern. Reports go to a human operator, who acts on them while protecting your privacy.

What happens if a rule is broken

Depending on severity, the operator may ask for a correction, restrict the profile, or remove it entirely from Kidan. Serious violations — fraud, harassment, abuse — mean immediate removal.

Why so strict? Because profiles here carry real people's trust. A rules-light space rewards the loudest; a rules-strong space protects the most honest.
