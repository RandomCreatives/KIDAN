Your data

Privacy notice

Kidan exists because dating platforms usually ask for far more of your dignity — and your data — than they need. This notice explains, in plain language, exactly what we collect, why, how it is protected, and what the law gives you as a right.

The principle behind everything here

Discovery on Kidan is anonymous by design: other members never see your photo or your name. The identifying information we do need (to confirm you're a real person applying seriously) is collected separately, encrypted, and kept visible only to the human operator who reviews profiles.

What we collect — and why

  • Telegram account reference (your internal Telegram user id) — needed simply to know which account is talking to the bot and to deliver messages. We do not receive your Telegram contacts or any of your chats.
  • Verification details — legal name, date of birth, phone number, and a one-time verification photo. Purpose: human identity verification before you appear in discovery, and reaching you if something needs a human reply.
  • Profile answers — your values, faith, character, and what you seek. Purpose: forming the anonymous values-shot profile others see and matching compatibility.
  • Journey activity — which guided journeys you start, your answers, and progression. Purpose: running the journey mechanism itself (including automatic next-step messages) and showing your status.
  • Technical logs — short-lived diagnostic records to keep the service running and debug faults. These never contain your profile answers or messages.
  • Anything you send us — feedback, questions, or a report filed from the Report a concern page. Purpose: resolving the request. If you file without an account, you may optionally leave a way to reach you.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We do not run advertising.

How it is stored

  • Your legal name, phone number, date of birth, and verification photo are encrypted at the field level — database storage holds ciphertext, not readable values.
  • Traffic between Telegram, the app, and our servers travels over TLS.
  • Operator access to decrypted identity is restricted to the small number of people charged with review, and is never placed in public or chat channels.

Who sees what

  • Other members see only your anonymous values-shot profile — never your photo, name, phone number, or raw answers.
  • The operator sees verification details (decrypted) while reviewing your profile, and sees your public code (like KD-48213X) afterwards.
  • No one else. Not advertisers, not data brokers, not other Telegram users.

How long we keep it

  • Verification photo: deleted after review, or reduced to a small thumbnail that is itself removed within 14 days of approval.
  • Profile & journey data: kept while your account is active, so the service functions.
  • Reports and feedback: kept while needed to resolve the matter, and for record-keeping after.
  • After account deletion: your data is removed except where we must retain minimal records for legal compliance.

What the law says — your rights

Kidan operates under Ethiopian law, including the Personal Data Protection Proclamation adopted in 2024. It, and standard international practice in data protection, give you the following rights over your personal information:

  • To know what data is held about you and why — that's what this page is for.
  • To access / export a copy of your data. You can request it straight from the app (Settings → Data) at any time.
  • To correct inaccurate information via your profile, or by contacting us.
  • To delete your data. Deleting your account in the app removes your profile, verification details, and journey data.
  • To object or complain about how your data is handled — to us first (via Report a concern, we genuinely want to hear it), and if unresolved, to the national authority supervising personal-data protection.
  • To be informed if a security breach puts your personal data at significant risk. We will notify affected members without undue delay.

The basis for our processing is your consent (asked clearly before your profile starts) plus the legitimate interest of running a safe, verified introduction service — never more, never marketing.

Cookies & tracking

The Telegram mini app and this site do not use advertising cookies, trackers, or analytics that identify you. The app stores what it needs to sign you in on your device only.

Contact

Questions about this notice, access/export/deletion requests, or complaints are all welcome through the report page (choose the “privacy” topic), or to the operator reachable from the bot's Support button.

The one-line version: everything identifying about you is encrypted, seen only by the human reviewer, never shown in discovery, exportable and deletable by you at any time.
