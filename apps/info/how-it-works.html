How Kidan works

Kidan is an introduction service, not a dating feed. Everything happens inside the Telegram bot and its mini app: you build a profile, a real person reviews it, and you only ever see other people as values-based, anonymous profiles.

1. Start with the bot

You open the Kidan bot on Telegram and tap Launch. The bot walks you through a short consent step — what we collect, why, and what we never do with it — before anything personal is asked.

2. Build your profile

In the mini app you answer questions about yourself and the values that matter to you: faith, family, character, ambitions, and what you're looking for. Two parts of this step are deliberately separated:

  • Verification details — your legal name, date of birth, phone number, and a verification photo. These exist so a human operator can confirm you're a real person applying seriously. They are encrypted, visible only to the reviewer, and never shown to other members.
  • Discovery profile — the part other members eventually see. It contains no photo and no name: just age range, values, and a private reference code (like KD-48213X).

3. Human review

A real person reviews every profile before it ever appears in discovery. Profiles that break the rules — dishonesty, disrespect, missing or blocked-out verification photos — are rejected with the reason shown to you, so you can fix and resubmit.

4. Discovery — without photos or names

Once approved, your discovery feed shows values-shot profiles: short cards that capture who someone is and what they care about. There are deliberately no photographs and no names at this stage. You decide on character and values first.

5. Passing the torch — the journey

When a profile interests you, you can start a guided journey: a structured, timed sequence of questions the two of you answer separately, revealing more of each other step by step. It's designed so neither side is rushed and both sides always know what happens next. If a journey ends in a mutual yes from both of you, the connection is confirmed.

6. The introduction

Only after both of you confirm are identities revealed to each other — never before, and never behind anyone's back. Contact details are not shared before this point; early conversation happens through the app's structured prompts, and personal contact information is private by default.

7. You stay in control

  • Your personal data stays inside the app — never in the chat, never on a profile card.
  • You can export a full copy of your data at any time from the app.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from Settings; requests and reports are also answered from this hub's report page.
In short: values first, photos and names last — and no introduction ever happens unless both people actively want it.
